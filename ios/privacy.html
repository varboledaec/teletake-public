TELETAKE FOR IPHONE

Privacy policy

TeleTake for iPhone · Victor Arboleda · Version 0.3.0 · Policy revision October 17, 2026

This page covers TeleTake for iPhone 0.3.0, currently in testing. Physical-device capture, speech recognition and network behavior remain under verification. No App Store availability or review approval is claimed.

Your content on your device

TeleTake stores scripts, copies of recorded or imported videos, captions, language choices, edit history and exports in its private app storage. It preserves the imported original and saves edits separately. Cutting a word or correcting a caption does not erase the original audio or earlier transcript archives.

TeleTake has no account registration, ads, in-app purchases, developer analytics SDK or developer-operated content synchronization. TeleTake is provided by Victor Arboleda.

Camera, microphone and speech

Camera and microphone permissions are used to record a take you start. You may deny or revoke them in iOS Settings. You can still write scripts, rehearse or import a selected video.

Caption generation uses Apple Speech after recording in the selected language. TeleTake checks on-device support and sets the recognition request to require on-device processing. If unavailable or if recognition fails, it stops and preserves your existing captions; it does not provide a cloud fallback. Language resources are managed by iOS.

Apple controls the availability of system speech languages and its system services. A support flag is not a guarantee that recognition will succeed on your phone. TeleTake does not translate or transcribe live. Review automatic text and timings before sharing.

Import, save and share

Apple’s selective Photos picker and Files picker let you choose a video. TeleTake copies it into private storage without requesting unrestricted Photos access, contacts or location.

Exports are prepared locally. When you choose Share and a destination, that app or storage provider receives the selected file and any metadata in it under its own policies. TeleTake does not publish videos automatically and cannot retract external copies. SRT and VTT contain text and timing; visual caption styling is included only in an exported video.

Tutorials

Six written guides are included in English and Spanish and readable offline. This build has no production tutorial videos or configured remote video hosts. Dayana’s planned videos are in English; Spanish narration is not included. Reading Help does not download a video or report lesson completion.

A future video download requires an explicit action and must disclose its host and size. Its host may receive IP and technical request data. Host handling and this policy must be reviewed before enabling production URLs. Included or downloaded videos are validated before use and cached privately.

Retention, Trash and backups

Scripts and takes remain until you remove them or remove the app. Deleting a script removes it from the active library. Moving a take to Trash retains the original, edits, archived transcripts and in-app exports; Trash does not expire automatically. Help → Manage local data lets you restore a take or permanently delete its Trash entry after confirmation.

Permanent deletion cannot be undone in TeleTake. It does not remove files already saved or shared elsewhere or copies in existing backups. iOS or computer backups may include app data according to your settings. Removing the app removes its local container; backups must be managed separately. There is no account-deletion workflow because TeleTake has no accounts.

The editor creates temporary rendered previews and removes its active preview when it closes normally. Interrupted processes may leave temporary files until iOS clears them. Speech extraction also uses temporary audio files. Storage and system backup policies are controlled by iOS.

Platform services and protection

Apple may process operating-system diagnostics or other system-service information according to its settings and policies. Local processing in TeleTake is not a promise about every service running on the phone.

TeleTake uses iOS app isolation and permission controls. It does not promise separate app-level encryption of every file or control over external copies, modified devices or receiving services. Future changes in hosting, billing or data handling require updated disclosures before release.

Contact

Developer: Victor Arboleda. For support or privacy requests, email info@easytechcommerce.com. Your email provider delivers the address, message and attachments you choose to send. Correspondence is used to handle your request; send only the information needed. Private scripts or recordings are not required.

Ask through the same contact if you want support correspondence removed, subject to applicable requirements. The developer cannot remotely access or erase content stored only in TeleTake on your phone. Opening the email action does not send a message or attach app files.

This policy is included in the iPhone app. Future changes to data handling will be described in an updated policy and relevant store disclosures.
