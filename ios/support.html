TELETAKE FOR IPHONE

Support guide

TeleTake for iPhone · Victor Arboleda · info@easytechcommerce.com

This page covers TeleTake for iPhone 0.3.0, currently in testing. Physical-device capture, speech recognition and network behavior remain under verification. No App Store availability or review approval is claimed.

App and speech languages

TeleTake follows the iPhone language and supports English and Spanish. Where available, choose the app language in iOS Settings → Apps → TeleTake → Language. Changing the interface does not translate saved scripts, captions or video audio. The spoken language for transcription is chosen separately in Words.

Start a take

Save a script in Scripts, reopen it and choose Rehearse & record. Adjust type size and pace, rehearse, then start recording after the countdown. Camera and microphone access are required to record. You can also import a video through Takes → Import → Files or Photos.

Create or correct captions

Open a take and choose the Words or Captions tab. Select English or Spanish and start on-device transcription. Availability depends on the phone and Apple’s installed language resources. If unavailable or recognition fails, use manual captions; existing captions are preserved.

Speech-language resources are managed by iOS. TeleTake does not provide cloud fallback, live transcription or translation. If recognition cannot start, you can enter captions manually. Review automatic text, word timing and chunk boundaries.

Edit and export

Use Timeline to split, trim or remove linked video and audio. Words lets you remove a spoken word or consecutive phrase with its video. Undo restores edits; the original remains separate.

Use Style for caption font, palette, size, position and emphasis, or a video look. Caption size and position save one edit when you finish a slider drag.

Export → MP4 with captions includes captions, cuts and appearance. MP4 without captions preserves cuts and appearance. SRT and VTT export text and timing only. Preview the actual MP4 before sharing it. Output is portrait 1080×1920; other aspect ratios are fitted with borders. Frame rate and codec compatibility depend on the source and device. HDR/color fidelity has not been validated.

Interrupted work or slow previews

Keep the app in the foreground while recording, transcribing or exporting. Moving it to the background stops the recording session and cancels active editor work; saved edits and the original are retained. A new preview is prepared when you return. Check any interrupted recording before sharing it.

Preparing preview renders a temporary MP4. Wait for it to finish before exporting. Large or long clips require additional storage and time; long-media and thermal behavior remain unverified.

Your files and help

Use Help → Manage local data for Trash, restore and recovery of unindexed originals. Cutting a section does not erase it from the original or prior recognition archives. Permanent deletion does not remove copies shared elsewhere or existing backups.

Six written guides in English and Spanish work offline. Production tutorial videos have not been added; no video download URL is configured. Dayana’s planned videos are in English; Spanish narration is not included.
