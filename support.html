TeleTake Support

TeleTake brings scripts, a front-camera teleprompter and editable captions to Android. It is free, with no ads, in-app purchases or account registration.

Contact

info@easytechcommerce.com

For help or privacy questions, email the address above. Include your TeleTake version from Settings, your Android version, your phone model and the steps that caused the issue. Send only the information needed. Do not include passwords or private recordings unless you choose to share a relevant sample.

Start a take

Save a script in Scripts. Open it, adjust the text size and scrolling speed, then record with the front camera and microphone. You can also import a video you choose through Android's file picker.

Prepare captions offline

English and Spanish speech models are included. Open Settings and choose Prepare beside the language you need, or follow the model prompt when generating captions. Setup works without internet. Keep at least 150 MB free during setup; prepared models use about 60 MB for Spanish or 71 MB for English. Speech recognition runs after recording and may make mistakes. Review the text and timing before sharing. Live transcription and translation are not included.

Change caption style

Open Takes → choose a take → Edit video → Captions → Caption style. Choose Sans, Serif or Mono, change Text size, or use Bold, Underline, Emphasis and Typing. Scroll within the lower panel to see all controls. Seek to a time where a caption is visible to preview your changes.

Export captions with their appearance

Inside Video editor, open Export → Export with captions. This includes the editor's captions, cuts and appearance settings in an MP4. Export without captions keeps the cuts and appearance without text. SRT and VTT files contain subtitle text and timing; they do not reproduce the visual styling of a captioned MP4. Use Android's Save or Share controls to choose the destination.

Media and preview

The editor and captioned MP4 export support portrait 9:16 videos. Appearance tools require SDR video. Codec support depends on your device. Keep the app open during transcription and export. If Preparing compatible preview appears, wait for the temporary edited preview to finish. Review the exported file before sharing it.

Your files and deletion

Your scripts, takes, edits and recognition data are stored in the app. Deleting a take removes its app-owned original, captions, edit project, history and recognition archives when deletion succeeds. Cutting a section or resetting an edit does not erase its original recording or recognition archives. If deletion reports an error, use Android's storage settings to remove remaining private app data. Save anything you want to keep before clearing app data or uninstalling. Copies already saved or shared elsewhere must be managed at their destination. TeleTake has no user accounts to delete. See the Privacy Policy for full retention details.

Included help and licenses

Open Settings → Help & tutorials for six written English guides. No tutorial videos are included in this version. Open Settings → Licenses for third-party software and model notices. Both speech models are provided under Apache 2.0.
